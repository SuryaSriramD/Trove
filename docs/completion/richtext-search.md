The original failure remains in [candidate evidence](development-candidate-be6fc81.md).

The same readable projection feeds search, AI evidence, proposal previews and personal-review record fields. Independent review caught the latter direct consumer; its separate failing regression is retained.

Search drains that durable queue before retrieval, replacing chunks, clearing obsolete embeddings through the existing sqlite-vec deletion triggers, and advancing the index generation. Canonical values, formatting, identities, revisions and history are unchanged. Current-version initialization does not repeatedly invalidate the index. Older/restored workspaces may pay a first-query rebuild cost; no canonical schema migration is required.
